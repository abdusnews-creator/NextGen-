Plans & Premium SEO

Choose the tools for your growth.

From essential creator tools to advanced SEO and an ultra-premium workspace. Basic includes 1 connected channel, Growth SEO includes 3 connected channels, and Ultra Premium includes up to 10 connected channels. Full SEO is ₹1,499/month; Ultra Premium is ₹4,999/month (proposed).

ESSENTIAL

Basic

₹499 / month · proposed

For creators who need help preparing content and basic metadata.

  • Content draft and caption helpers
  • Basic hashtags and tags
  • Manual review checklist
  • 1 connected platform account: YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram
  • Full SEO research and premium scoring
  • Advanced competitor SEO insights
FULL SEO · PREMIUM

Growth SEO

₹1,499 / month · proposed

For creators who want the complete SEO workflow where data access is supported.

  • Everything in Basic
  • Up to 3 connected accounts across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Threads
  • Keyword and related-search research
  • Title, description and tag optimization
  • SEO checklist and premium scoring
  • Competitor insights where available
  • Post-publication SEO review where authorized data exists
ULTRA PREMIUM

Ultra Premium

₹4,999 / month · proposed

A top-tier package for serious creators, teams and multi-channel workflows.

  • Everything in Growth SEO
  • Up to 10 connected accounts across YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Telegram, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X and other supported services
  • Higher usage limits, subject to provider costs
  • Multi-channel and team workflow priority, once built
  • Advanced reporting and performance reviews, where data is available
  • Priority support, if offered
  • No guaranteed views, virality or earnings
